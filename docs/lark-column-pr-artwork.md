# Lark固定コラム PR画像の制作記録

内蔵の画像生成機能を使用し、写真と日本語コピーを組み合わせたPR画像を3枚作成。人物は架空のイメージであり、SFLの実際の受講者・社員の写真ではありません。原画像をWebPへ変換し、見出しの文字を含む画像全体を表示します。

## 保存先

- `public/images/columns/lark-free-start.webp`
- `public/images/columns/lark-bid-workflow.webp`
- `public/images/columns/lark-business-growth.webp`

## 最終生成プロンプト

### lark-free-start

```text
Use case: ads-marketing. Create ONE finished Japanese corporate PR banner, landscape 16:9. It will be a horizontal featured-article cover in SFL's public-procurement business portal. This must look like a polished commercial campaign by a professional Japanese art director: photorealistic editorial business photography, very large impeccably typeset Japanese copy, assertive graphic layout, strong blue/navy/cyan palette, restrained accent color, excellent contrast. NOT an illustration, NOT a paper-cut scene, NOT a cartoon, NOT a 3D mascot, NOT a website mockup. Human subjects are fictional Japanese adults, naturally photographed, realistic hands, confident and approachable, professional everyday attire. Text should be genuinely legible even at thumbnail size. All exact text below only, no extra text or tiny fake UI words, no watermarks, no fake logos, no partner/certification claims. Maintain a 6% safe margin all sides. The company credit is plain text, not an invented symbol. Don't include AEON, TikTok or ByteDance logos or imply endorsements.
Design: luminous white and pale cyan background, a photograph of a professional Japanese woman in her 30s happily working on a laptop on the RIGHT third. The LEFT 60% carries bold large dark navy typography. Add tasteful blue diagonal branding blocks and one vivid yellow underline accent. A striking advertising composition with a clear focal headline.
Text, verbatim:
Top label: "はじめてのLark"
Dominant headline in two lines: "無料から、" / "仕事が変わる。"
Supporting line: "案件管理・資料・報連相をひとつに"
Small bottom qualification: "Starterプラン・機能に上限あり"
Company credit: "合同会社SFL"
Make the headline the strongest visual element, photographer quality, commercial retouching, no outlined pseudo-icons.
```
