# コラム見出し画像の制作記録

合同会社SFLのお役立ち情報向けに、内蔵の画像生成機能で6枚を新規作成。横型16:9のイラストをWebP形式に変換して掲載。日本語見出しは読みやすさと拡大表示のためサイト上のテキストとして重ねています。

## 最終生成プロンプト

### new-business-risk

```text
Use case: ads-marketing. Create ONE original editorial illustration to be used as a wide horizontal article header for the Japanese company SFL's public procurement learning portal. Landscape aspect ratio 16:9. Professional, beautifully composed dimensional paper-cut illustration with refined matte surfaces, soft natural shadows, and very light ivory / pale blue background. Deep navy, cornflower blue, muted teal and small warm amber accents. Restrained, sophisticated, approachable business education aesthetic. Crisp silhouettes, no cartoon faces. COMPOSITION: the LEFT 52% of the canvas must be clean, nearly-white negative space for readable Japanese HTML headline overlay; all illustrative objects in the RIGHT 48%, with generous margins and fully visible subjects. Balanced cohesive series. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no borders. This is a finished website image, not a mockup of a webpage.
SUBJECT FOR THIS IMAGE: A thoughtful business readiness still life: a navy-blue clipboard with three large checkmark shapes, a small protective shield, and a neatly stacked set of ivory documents, with a tiny amber caution triangle. Visual theme: checking delivery capability, funding and entry requirements before taking on public-sector contracts. Avoid money flying, distress, or frightening danger.
```
